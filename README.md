# Smart Street Light- Crime Risk Response System
AI-Driven Smart Street Light with Crime Risk Response System  An intelligent IoT and AI-based smart street lighting system designed to improve public safety by detecting suspicious activities and dynamically responding to different crime-risk levels.  The system uses a USB webcam connected to a laptop** for real-time video monitoring and **YOLO-based computer vision** for detecting people, vehicles, and potential threats. Motion and behavioral information can be analyzed to classify situations into **Low, Medium, or High Risk** levels.  An **ESP32 microcontroller** controls the physical street-light prototype, including the LED lighting and buzzer, according to the risk level detected by the AI system. **PIR and LDR sensors** provide motion and ambient-light information for smarter and energy-efficient lighting.  ### Key Features - 🎥 Real-time video surveillance using a USB webcam - 🤖 YOLO-based person, vehicle, and object detection - 🧍 Human activity and movement analysis - 📊 Crime-risk classification: **Low, Medium & High** - 💡 Automatic street-light brightness control - 🚨 Buzzer activation during high-risk situations - 🌙 LDR-based day/night detection - 🚶 PIR-based motion detection - 🔌 Python-to-ESP32 serial communication - 📸 Capability to capture evidence during suspicious events - ⚡ Energy-efficient lighting during normal/low-risk conditions  ### Risk Response  **LOW Risk →** Dim street light (~20%) + Buzzer OFF   **MEDIUM Risk →** Bright street light + Buzzer OFF   **HIGH Risk →** Bright street light + Buzzer ON + Evidence capture/alert  ### Technology Stack  **AI / Computer Vision:** Python, YOLO, OpenCV, MediaPipe   **Hardware:** ESP32 DevKitC, Logitech C270 USB Webcam, PIR Sensor, LDR Sensor, LED, Piezo Buzzer   **Communication:** USB Serial Communication   **Development:** Python, Arduino IDE  ### Objective  The project aims to transform conventional street lights into **intelligent public-safety infrastructure** capable of monitoring their surroundings, identifying potentially dangerous situations, responding automatically, and assisting authorities through real-time alerts and captured evidence.  It combines **Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, IoT, and embedded systems** to create a scalable approach toward safer and smarter urban environments.
